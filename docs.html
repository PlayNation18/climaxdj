Documentation

ClimaxDJ 4.3.0 ships three product worlds in one Windows app.

01 — DJ System

Decks (2 or 4), mixer, waveforms, Auto-DJ, library, pads, MIDI, recording. Toggle 2 Decks / 4 Decks in the toolbar.

02 — Studio

Open DAW. Modes: PAT, SONG, SESSION. Piano Roll, step sequencer, synths (Sub-Bass, Supersaw Lead, Warm Pad, Pluck, E-Piano, Acid 303), drum sampler/slicer, mixer, automation, WAV & stems export, VST3.

03 — Engine

WASAPI output from ClimaxDJ. Optional ASIO bridge DLL for other hosts. MIDI learn. VST3 scan/host (desktop). Stem KI engine optional download.
