    Built for the booth

    Everything you need to mix

    Real capabilities from ClimaxDJ 4.3.0 — the same stack you’d expect next to VirtualDJ, Serato or rekordbox.

    Professional Mixing

    Dual decks, crossfader modes, EQ, filter, Sync, Slip and Auto DJ with soft fades — tempo and pitch stay original.

    Intelligent BPM Analysis

    Background analysis tags BPM, Camelot key and energy so the crate stays mix-ready.

    Real-Time Waveforms

    Scrolling multi-band waveforms on each deck with beatgrid, zoom and hotcue markers.

    Advanced Audio Effects

    Reverb, delay, echo, filter, brake and slicer on the decks — plus mixer drive, comp and hall in the DAW.

    Loops & Samples

    Performance pads for Hot, Loop, Roll, Jump and Sampler — with a DAW slicer for precise chops.

    AI-Powered Features

    4-stem separation (vocals, drums, bass, melody) via Demucs, plus smart harmonic suggestions.

    MIDI Controller Support

    MIDI learn and ready mapping for Pioneer DDJ-FLX4 — play, cue, sync, EQ, faders, crossfader, hotcues.

    Professional Recording

    Record the DJ master or mic/line into the DAW as WAV or MP3 — plus stem and song export.

    Feel the signal
